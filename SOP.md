#### Statement of Purpose 2026

The Mt. Spokane Landowners Group and its associated online portal ***will***:

* **Serve** as a central point for property owners to communicate, collaborate, and advocate for the long-term stewardship of the Snowblaze recreational plat, in lieu of a functioning formal association
* **Promote** voluntary stewardship of shared infrastructure, including easements, drainage systems, and the privately maintained access road
* **Support** wildfire risk reduction through fuels mitigation, adherence to burn restrictions, and other preventative actions
* **Curate** and disseminate an annual Best Practices document for property owners, addressing topics such as off-grid cabin development on steep, fire-prone, forested terrain with high snow loads, as well as general guidance on road etiquette, waste disposal, and land use
* **Maintain** clear and constructive communication with adjacent and related organizations, including those with recorded easements through the plat, such as the Snowblaze Condominium Association, Mt. Spokane State Park, and Inland Empire Paper Company
* **Act** in an advisory capacity if a formal landowners association is re-established, supporting the transition and advocating for updates to the plat structure and bylaws that reflect current use cases and conditions

The Mt. Spokane Landowners Group and its associated online portal ***will not***:

* **Raise, spend, or hold funds** for capital improvements. Projects requiring funding will be organized as volunteer efforts, with any fundraising conducted through third-party service providers (e.g., GoFundMe)
* **Enforce** rules through punitive measures or fines related to perceived infractions by property owners
* **Assume** any legal authority, governance role, or formal responsibility whatsoever
